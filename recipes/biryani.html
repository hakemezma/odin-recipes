Chicken Biryani

Discription

traditional indian dish containing
chicken, rice, kishmish, sharya,
it tastes so good

ingredients

  • middle sized chicken
  • 200g rice
  • 100g potato
  • 75g kishmish
  • 50g Shareyah
  • spices as needed
Steps
  1. cook the chicken in hot water for about 20 minutes
  2. put the rice in nomal temp water for about the same timing
  3. mix all the ingredients together into one pan
